Who's baking

Two sisters, one small kitchen

ButterBlues started the way most home bakeries do — one too many birthday cakes for friends, and a growing suspicion that we should probably be charging for them.

We still bake out of the same kitchen, in batches small enough that one of us can taste everything before it goes into a box. No commercial mixers, no premixes, no shortcuts we'd be embarrassed to explain. Butter, good chocolate, real vanilla, and family recipes older than most of our customers' relationships with us.

  • 100% eggless, every single bake — no exceptions
  • Baked to order, never held in stock or frozen ahead
  • Butter and cream, never vegetable shortening
  • Boxes and sleeves we'd be happy to receive ourselves
  • An honest answer when something isn't right for your occasion
The nicest thing anyone says to us is that the box looked like someone made it, not bought it.
